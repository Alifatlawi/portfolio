# Surface brief: portfolio home

**Scope**: the single-page portfolio at `/` (`app/(portfolio)/page.tsx`, its layout, `components/*` outside `components/murafa`). The `/murafa` product site is out of scope.

**Visitor mode**: Experience. The work itself leads from the first viewport.

**Audience and job**: recruiters and engineering managers scanning for 30–90 seconds (see PRODUCT.md). Action: start a conversation (contact form, email, LinkedIn).

**Proof on hand**: four live App Store apps with real screens (`public/work/<app>/`), captures of orka.store and movie.alialfatlawi.com (`public/work/sites/`), the TAD and Target descriptions, and the Murafa'a product site.

**User decisions (2026-10-21)**: work-led first screen; App Store and live-site imagery; hero copy reads "builds apps that ship"; the CV link is hidden while `public/CV.pdf` is empty; project years follow App Store release dates; Atilim Community is not listed; Altadark Group and Altadark Cloud stay listed but unlinked and not claimed "Live" (their sites are down); tadedu.com stays linked.

**Memorable moment**: the four real app screens settle onto one baseline beneath your name, set in two scripts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the portfolio as a bilingual modernist print sheet. Latin hangs from the left margin, Arabic from the right, and the shipped apps stand on the shared baseline between them. It refuses the name-hero-over-card-grid developer page and the cream, hairline and mono-label editorial look.

OWN-WORLD: flat spot inks on uncoated stock. A lapis field (#22389A family) owns the hero, the header band and the contact close. Bright neutral paper carries the reading sections. Saffron is the single second ink (action, active, overprint), and ink-black sets the text. Reem Kufi sets display in both scripts; Readex Pro sets text and labels. Hairline rules form a strict baseline grid, with square corners except the phone screens.

STORY: the visitor sees four real apps first, learns they were built by one engineer whose work is often Arabic-first, reads the flagship and web work as a set of plates, believes it from real screens and dates, then writes to Ali.

FIRST VIEWPORT: a full-width lapis field. "Ali Al-Fatlawi" runs at billboard scale from the left margin; علي الفتلاوي sits in saffron at the right margin on the next line. Lower left: "builds apps that ship." with the lede, the saffron "Start a conversation" action and facts. Lower right: four app screens stand on one full-width paper baseline, each with a label plate (name, year, platform, App Store link).

FORM: bilingual modernist print, position 3 of 7 on the ordered list; seed: 5b8e47fda74fb6ae.

Raises carried into the build:
- From the cassette deck fascia: every project carries the same label plate (status, platform, year, link), each field in a fixed position.
- From Greiman: scale jumps from footnote to billboard. Each section's Arabic title is set at wall scale beside its English heading, and numerals go billboard-size only where they carry data.
- From the silk canopy: two inks overprint where they cross; saffron over lapis is the only active and hover state, and it is always labeled in words as well.
- From the glaze shelf: all screens stand on one shared baseline at true relative scale, each with its recipe (stack, year) beside it.
- From the event display: pointing at or focusing a project isolates it and dims the rest, and its screens open in a pinned viewer.
- From the BBS: one baseline grid governs both scripts and every element, with full keyboard reach.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
